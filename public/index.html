A library that grows together.

Metachlorian users can contribute machine analysis from newly imported YouTube videos. Sharing is enabled by default and can be turned off in Community sharing settings. Warning: private or unlisted YouTube imports can publish metadata too. Turn sharing off before importing them if you want that metadata to stay private. Local and personal files are excluded. No videos, frames or face identities are uploaded.

Analysis is a starting point.

Descriptions are supplied by the community and may contain mistakes. A search result is not permission to reuse footage. Check the original video and its rights. Video visibility is not checked. Metadata may remain searchable after a video becomes private or is deleted. Previously published metadata may be removed by the operator.
